Thao Amelia Pham

I work on problems of multi-agent safety and security, especially concerning loss-of-control from unsafe interactions between multi-agent advanced AI agents. My current research focuses on red-teaming AI agent swarms in the wild to detect attacks that undermine current monitoring techniques in internal deployment settings.

I'm also broadly interested in generalization of misalignment in multi-agent RL training, and how to detect increasingly covert actions and reward hacking behaviors to robustly scale oversight.
